func haloRustCreationValue(v reflect.Value)any{switch v.Kind(){case reflect.Struct:out:=map[string]any{};for i:=0;i<v.NumField();i++{out[v.Type().Field(i).Name]=haloRustCreationValue(v.Field(i))};return out;case reflect.Array,reflect.Slice:if v.Kind()==reflect.Slice&&v.IsNil(){return nil};out:=[]any{};for i:=0;i<v.Len();i++{out=append(out,haloRustCreationValue(v.Index(i)))};return out;case reflect.Float32:return math.Float32bits(v.Interface().(float32));case reflect.Int:return v.Int();case reflect.Uint32,reflect.Uint64:return v.Uint();case reflect.Bool:return v.Bool()};panic(v.Kind())}
func haloRustWorldDump(s WorldObjectScan)any{creations:=[]any{};for _,c:=range s.Creations{creations=append(creations,haloRustCreationValue(reflect.ValueOf(c)))};return map[string]any{"scanned":s.Scanned,"creations":creations,"stats":haloRustCreationValue(reflect.ValueOf(s.Stats)),"keyframes":haloRustWorldKeyframes(s),"tracks":haloRustWorldTracks(s)}}

func haloRustWorldKeyframes(s WorldObjectScan)any{times:=s.Keyframes.TimesUS;if times==nil{times=[]uint64{}};keys:=[]types.EquipmentLifeKey{};for k:=range s.Keyframes.SeenUS{keys=append(keys,k)};sort.Slice(keys,func(i,j int)bool{if keys[i].Slot!=keys[j].Slot{return keys[i].Slot<keys[j].Slot};return keys[i].Gen<keys[j].Gen});seen:=[]any{};for _,k:=range keys{ts:=s.Keyframes.SeenUS[k];if ts==nil{ts=[]uint64{}};seen=append(seen,map[string]any{"slot":k.Slot,"generation":k.Gen,"times":ts})};return map[string]any{"times":times,"seen":seen}}
func haloRustWorldTracks(s WorldObjectScan)any{tracks:=[]any{};for _,tr:=range s.Tracks{var points any;if tr.Pts!=nil{pts:=[]any{};for _,p:=range tr.Pts{pts=append(pts,map[string]any{"timestamp_us":p.TimestampUS,"chunk":p.Chunk,"bits":[]uint32{math.Float32bits(p.X),math.Float32bits(p.Y),math.Float32bits(p.Z)},"at_rest":p.AtRest})};points=pts};tracks=append(tracks,map[string]any{"slot":tr.Slot,"generation":tr.Gen,"points":points})};return tracks}
