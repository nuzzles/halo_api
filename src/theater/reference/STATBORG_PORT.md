Constant mapping: scanner ID14, generation2/value2, header5, max round7, dense
mask64, max components58, sparse index6/count1..7, slot6..24 and team maximum8,
tail64, record cap33,076, mode score0..250, and A/B counter range[-2^20,2^20].
The decoder retains these widths/checks; the source cap is explicitly named.
Round-admission constants are now named and directly compared with Go: minimum
strict run3, minimum player records25, relative share10%, maximum round7 and one
consecutive empty round. Counts are over player records; coherent score runs may
come from player or team slots. Equal timestamps retain input ordering.

The resolver now composes the native helper results, preserving the previous
written/real/contradicted/decreed publication. Returning helper results exposes
native intermediate evidence that the earlier fused resolver discarded. Boolean
maps are represented as sets of their true keys; false entries do not contribute,
as verified by the independently supplied map cases.

The new helper oracle is `halo_rust_round_helpers_test.go.txt` /
`round-helpers-v41.json.zlib` and is registered in `generate_oracles.py`. Tests compare
native outputs, not expectations generated by Rust. Loaded and captured evidence
is detailed in PORT_STATUS.md. Pending goal-wide concerns still include reader
context/profile APIs, complete document propagation and positive captured objective
mode gates; none is waived by this source-file audit.
