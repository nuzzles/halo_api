native refTronquee marker at source_bits + 1. This marker is diagnostic, not a
consumed bit or a valid next-record boundary. No absent EventReference is fabricated
from a truncated reference: DecodedHeadEvent retains its complete prefix, a
Truncated stop and that marker. The original chunk bytes retain the failed region.

oracles remain required checks. The legacy band-based vehicle scanner has a
separate fallible API; its abort-on-refusal policy is not changed by this native
head-reader migration and is not a claim about reconstructed player timelines.
