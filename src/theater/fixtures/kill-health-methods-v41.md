# Kill-source health and publication method oracle

Reference: LevelUp `43a01721e8a02c87c955e175936f0ccf8dd97a81`.
Generator: `../reference/halo_rust_kill_health_test.go.txt`, registered in
`generate_oracles.py` as `TestHaloRustKillHealth` in `internal/facts/killsource`.

The fixture calls every native KillSourceHealth method, PathStats.Ratio and
Result.LineByLinePublishable. Its 2,800 health states combine zero/small/large
candidate populations, both sides of the 18% and 36% thresholds, incomplete
coverage, nonpositive real-death denominators, outside-roster counts, and
independent walk/scan catalog misses. Coverage cases are crossed independently
with every counter/flag case to avoid coupling a warning flag to the denominator. Each state has six publication controls:
negative/zero/positive bijection margin crossed with determined/undetermined.
All three native verdicts are required. Ratios compare as f64 bits; diagnostic
strings and metric pairs compare in order. Native nil diagnostic lists normalize
to empty Rust vectors. No expected behavior is generated by the Rust code.

The counters other than DeathsReal are nonnegative, matching these parsed-count
outputs. This does not establish native behavior for arbitrary negative
hand-constructed counters or integer overflow. The oracle checks publication
policy, not whether the policy is scientifically sufficient for every film.
No parser output or recording is generated by this method fixture.
